1.2 Creating an Account

Step 1: Navigate to your role-specific login page.
Step 2: Click "Register" to switch to the registration form.
Step 3: Fill in your details:
  • Email — Your email address (must be unique)
  • Password — Minimum 6 characters
  • Full Name — Your full legal name
  • Phone — Contact number
  • Location — Country, State, City (auto-detected from your IP, editable)
  • Institute — Your educational institution
  • Experience Level — Your current skill level
Step 4: Click "Register". You'll be redirected to the login page.
Your location is auto-detected when you visit the registration page. You can manually change it using the dropdown menus.

1.3 Logging In

Step 1: Enter your registered email and password.
Step 2: Click "Login".
Step 3: You'll be taken to your role-specific dashboard.
If your account has been deactivated or login is disabled by an admin, you will see an error message. Contact your administrator.

1.4 Dashboard Navigation

The portal uses a sidebar menu on the left side of the screen. Click any menu item to switch between sections. The sidebar can be customized in Settings.

2.3 Accessing Courses

Step 1: Click "Courses" in the sidebar.
Step 2: You'll see all courses available to your enrolled batches.
Step 3: Click a course to expand its topics.
Step 4: Each topic lists YouTube videos — click to watch.

2.4 Submitting Assignments

Step 1: Click "Assignments" in the sidebar.
Step 2: You'll see assignments grouped by batch with due dates.
Step 3: Click "Upload" next to an assignment.
Step 4: Select your file. Accepted types: .sql, .py, .txt, .xls, .xlsx (max 50 MB).
Step 5: Wait for the upload to complete. A success message will appear.
Only the file types listed above are accepted. Other file formats will be rejected.

2.5 Requesting IP Whitelist

Step 1: Click "IP Requests" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Fill in the form: IP address, port number, protocol (TCP/UDP), and reason.
Step 3: Submit the request. Status starts as "Pending".
Step 4: Wait for admin approval. Once approved, your IP is automatically added to the server firewall.
Your current IP address is often auto-detected. Make sure it matches where you'll connect from.

2.6 Viewing Credentials

Step 1: Click "Credentials" in the sidebar.
Step 2: You'll see database credentials assigned to your batches/courses.
Step 3: Click "Reveal Password" to show the password. This action is logged for security.

2.7 Payments & Agreements

  • Payments — View your payment history, amounts, due dates, and status (pending/partial/paid/overdue).
  • Agreements — View training agreements. Click "Sign" to digitally sign an agreement sent by admin.

3.3 Creating a Batch

Step 1: Click "Batch Manager" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Enter a batch name (e.g., "SQL Batch - March 2026").
Step 3: Optionally add a Zoom meeting link.
Step 4: Click "Create Batch".
Step 5: Your batch appears in the list below. You can update the Zoom link any time.

3.4 Managing Assignments

Step 1: Click "Assignments" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Select a batch from the dropdown.
Step 3: Click "+ New" to create an assignment with title, instructions, and optional due date.
Step 4: View student submissions by clicking the assignment. Download submitted files directly.

3.5 Managing Course Videos

Step 1: Click "Course Videos" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Select a batch.
Step 3: Click "+ Add Video" and provide: title, YouTube URL, and optional description.
Step 4: Videos appear in a card grid. You can edit or delete them.

3.6 Distributing Materials

Step 1: Click "Batch Materials" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Select a batch.
Step 3: Click "+ Add Material". Choose type:
  • File upload — PDF, DOC, SQL, ZIP (uploaded to S3)
  • External link — URL to external resource
Step 4: Set title, description, and whether it's view-only or downloadable.
Step 5: Optionally link to a specific course or topic.

3.7 Assigning Projects to Students

Step 1: Click "Student Projects" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Select a batch from the dropdown.
Step 3: Click "+ Assign Project".
Step 4: Select a student from your batch and a project (created by admin).
Step 5: Fill in: role (e.g., "Full Stack Developer"), start date, end date, and description.
Step 6: Click "Assign". The project will automatically appear on the student's resume.
Projects are created by admins in the Business Context section. You can only assign existing projects to students in your own batches.

4.3 Managing Students

View all students: Click "Students" → see list with name, email, batches, status.
Edit student: Click a student → modify fields like active status, login enabled, notes.
Add to batch: In student detail → select a batch and click "Add to Batch".
Remove from batch: Click ✕ next to a batch enrollment.
Deactivate: Toggle "Active" to disable the account.

4.4 Managing Programs & Courses

Create Program: Programs → + New → enter title, code, duration, category, dates → associate courses.
Create Course: Courses → + New → enter title, code, level → add topics → add YouTube videos per topic.
Link to Batch: Programs can be linked to batches during batch creation.

4.5 Approving IP Requests

Step 1: Click "IP Requests" in the sidebar.
Step 2: Review pending requests (student name, IP, port, reason).
Step 3: Click "Approve" to add the IP to the AWS Security Group automatically, or "Reject" with a note.
Step 4: The action is recorded in audit logs.
Approving an IP request immediately modifies the AWS EC2 Security Group. Ensure the IP and port are valid before approving.

4.6 Managing Credentials

Create Credential: Credentials → + New → fill in type (PostgreSQL/AWS/Snowflake/MSSQL), host, port, database, username, password, region, notes, expiry date.
Assign Access: Select credential → assign to batch, course, or program. Only students in those scopes can view it.
Toggle Visibility: Set allow_student_view and allow_trainer_view flags.

4.7 Audit Logs

Step 1: Click "Audit Logs" in the sidebar.
Step 2: View chronological log of all system actions.
Step 3: Filter by action type, entity type, or date range.
Step 4: Each entry shows: timestamp, actor, action, entity, and metadata details.

5. ATS Resume Builder & DOCX Download

STUDENT

5.1 Overview

The ATS Resume Builder helps you create a professional, Applicant Tracking System-friendly resume. It has three tabs:

✏️

Editor Tab

Add and edit your resume content: profile links, education, work experience, skills, and personal projects. Assigned business projects appear automatically and cannot be edited.

👁️

Preview Tab

See a live preview of how your resume looks. All sections are rendered in a clean, ATS-optimized format with proper headings and formatting.

📥

DOCX Download Tab

Choose from 5 professionally designed templates and download your resume as a Microsoft Word document (.docx).

5.2 Building Your Resume

Step 1: Click "ATS Resume" in the sidebar.
Step 2: In the Editor tab, fill in each section:
  • Profile — LinkedIn URL, GitHub URL, portfolio URL, preferred job role, work authorization
  • Education — Add entries: institution, degree, field of study, start/end year, grade
  • Experience — Add entries: company, job title, start/end date, description of responsibilities
  • Skills — Add skills with proficiency level
  • Projects — Add personal projects: title, description, tech stack, URL
Step 3: Assigned Projects (marked with a blue border) appear automatically — these are real-world projects assigned by your trainer and are read-only.
Step 4: Switch to the Preview tab to see your formatted resume.
Step 5: All changes are auto-saved.

5.3 Downloading DOCX Resume

Step 1: Click the "📥 DOCX" tab.
Step 2: Choose a template by clicking one of the 5 cards:
TemplateBest ForStyle
📜 ClassicTraditional industriesTimes New Roman, centered header, formal borders
🔷 ModernTech & corporateCalibri, navy blue accents, clean lines
◻️ MinimalMaximum ATS compatibilityArial, no borders, whitespace-focused
🏛 ExecutiveSenior positionsGeorgia serif, bold headers, larger sizing
🎨 CreativeDesign & marketingCalibri, blue accents, modern layout
Step 3: Optionally update your Professional Summary text in the textarea.
Step 4: Click the green "📥 Download [Template] DOCX" button.
Step 5: The file downloads automatically as resume_[template].docx.
The "Minimal" template is recommended for maximum ATS compatibility. It uses the simplest formatting that parsers handle best.

5.4 What's Included in the DOCX

  • ✅ Your full name and contact information
  • ✅ Professional summary (from the text area)
  • ✅ Work experience with company, title, dates, and descriptions
  • ✅ All projects — both personal AND assigned business projects (merged together)
  • ✅ Education history
  • ✅ Skills list
  • ✅ Preferred role and LinkedIn/GitHub URLs

8. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Currently, password reset is handled by your administrator. Contact the admin to have your password updated manually.
Why can't I see any courses or batches?
You need to be enrolled in a batch by the trainer or admin. Contact your trainer to be added to the appropriate batch.
What file types can I upload for assignments?
Accepted file types: .sql, .py, .txt, .xls, .xlsx. Maximum file size is 50 MB.
How does the IP whitelist work?
When you submit an IP request, the admin reviews it. If approved, your IP address is automatically added to the AWS EC2 Security Group, allowing your connection to the training servers on the specified port.
Can I edit projects assigned by my trainer?
No. Assigned business projects are read-only and managed by your trainer or admin. They are automatically included in your resume. You can add your own personal projects separately.
Which resume template should I choose?
  • Minimal — Best for ATS systems (maximum compatibility)
  • Modern — Great for tech companies
  • Classic — Best for traditional industries (finance, healthcare)
  • Executive — Best for senior-level positions
  • Creative — Best for design/marketing roles
How do I access database credentials?
Go to "Credentials" in the sidebar. You'll see credentials assigned to your batches/courses. Click "Reveal Password" to show the password — this action is logged for security.
Can I sign an agreement digitally?
Yes. When an admin sends you an agreement, it will appear in the "Agreements" section with a "Sign" button. Clicking it records your digital signature with timestamp.
How are notification sent?
Trainers and admins can send notifications visible in your "Notifications" section. Unread notifications are highlighted. You can mark them as read.
How do I join a Zoom session?
Zoom links are displayed on your dashboard overview next to each batch. Click the "📹 Join Zoom" button to open the meeting in a new tab.
